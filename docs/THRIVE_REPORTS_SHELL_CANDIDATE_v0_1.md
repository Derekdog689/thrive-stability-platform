# THRIVE Reports Shell Candidate v0.1

## Status

Review-only participant product candidate.

## Purpose

Create the Reports area early so historical bank records have a stable home.

## Initial report families

### Monthly financial summary

- statement period
- source
- planned amount
- spent amount
- remaining amount
- imported transaction count
- explanation completion

### Annual financial history

- statement months available
- missing-month notices
- yearly totals
- category totals
- source coverage
- reconciliation status

### Budget history

- planned versus actual by period
- protected, flexible, support, and reserve categories
- archived plans

### Wellness and spending patterns

Later, after consented and reliable data exists:

- routine consistency
- self-reported stress
- sleep
- energy
- spending categories
- participant explanations

These are patterns, not diagnoses or conclusions.

### Trust comparison

Later and separately authorized:

- Trust-reported amount or action
- participant-reported amount or understanding
- difference
- participant message or request
- Trust response

## Initial shell behavior

The first shell may show:

- report cards;
- month and year selectors;
- coverage status;
- unavailable-state messages;
- no export or sharing until approved.

## Data boundaries

Reports must label each source:

- Bank source
- THRIVE participant entry
- THRIVE calculation
- Trust-reported fact
- Staff note

## Frozen boundary

No report route, export, sharing, or Trust comparison should be installed in this gate.
