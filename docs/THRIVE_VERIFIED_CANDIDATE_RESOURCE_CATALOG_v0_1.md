## Purpose

Create the first finite, source-grounded candidate catalog for THRIVE without turning Resources into a generic directory.

This pass intentionally stays below the approved 40-resource cap. It favors high-confidence national, Florida, Palm Beach County, and Broward County starting points that fit the current participant population and the Living Signal / Recovery Support proving direction.

No item in this document is authorized for database installation merely because it appears here.

## Verification posture

Candidate sources were checked against current official or institutional pages on 2026-10-01.

Priority remains:

1. official government / program authority;
2. official service-provider organization;
3. recognized nonprofit or institutional authority that actually provides or maintains the service.

A live URL is not enough for activation. Each installed Resource must still receive its canonical Resource record, authority relationship, active access path, participant guidance, verification record, geography/audience description, and workspace visibility decision.

## Recovery Support priority subset

For the first functional proving slice, use only the smallest high-confidence recovery subset:

1. Alcoholics Anonymous official meeting path;
2. Narcotics Anonymous official meeting path;
3. SMART Recovery meeting finder;
4. SAMHSA Find Support as a broad fallback/support gateway.

Do not mix all 21 candidates into the first participant experience.

The proving slice should answer:

> “I want to find recovery support. Show me real options I can choose from.”

## Source/use boundary

THRIVE may explain:

- what the resource is;
- who maintains it;
- how to reach it;
- what the participant can do there;
- what basic preparation may be useful when directly supported by the source.

THRIVE must not convert the presence of a Resource into:

- eligibility;
- recommendation;
- diagnosis;
- urgency;
- priority;
- relapse inference;
- treatment matching;
- legal conclusion.
