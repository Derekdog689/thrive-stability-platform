## Resource-versus-guidance rule

Not every useful outside-world link deserves a new canonical Resource.

Prefer an additional access path or guidance section when:
- the authority is already represented by an existing Resource;
- the participant need is a narrower job inside that authority;
- splitting the record would create duplicate verification and maintenance work.

Examples:
- SSA card replacement belongs under Social Security Administration.
- Goal barriers / if-then planning belongs under SMART Goal Setting Guide.

Create a separate canonical Resource when:
- it represents a distinct authority or service;
- it answers a distinct participant job;
- it requires separate verification, lifecycle, service-area, or visibility handling.

## Contextual routing principle

The canonical category describes **what the Resource is**.

THRIVE lanes describe **why the Resource is useful right now**.

Examples:
- GED is employment_education but may surface from Goals.
- CFPB savings guidance is financial_education but may surface from Money or Goals.
- Broward Transit is transportation but may surface because of employment, recovery meetings, appointments, or another participant-selected need.
- MyPlate is health_wellness_navigation but may surface from Wellness, Money, or a healthy-routine Goal.
- Birth-certificate replacement is identification_documents but may surface because a job, benefits application, housing step, or education goal is blocked.
