## Rejection note

Rejected on 2026-10-06 because the first 40 Resources are intended to support the full THRIVE environment, not expand Recovery from six to forty. The research below is retained as future scale runway only.

## Purpose

Expand the current six live Recovery resources toward the working target of roughly forty total Recovery entries without changing schema, authority boundaries, or participant behavior.

The existing live Recovery catalog remains the baseline and is not duplicated here:
- A.A. Daily Reflections
- Alcoholics Anonymous Meeting Support
- Celebrate Recovery Group Support
- NA Recovery Literature
- Narcotics Anonymous Meeting Support
- SMART Recovery Meeting Finder

This candidate proposes **34 additional entries**. The catalog is intentionally mixed across:
- meeting and community finders;
- online/remote access;
- family and loved-one support;
- newcomer orientation;
- official literature and practical tools;
- secular, spiritual, Buddhist-inspired, and 12-Step pathways;
- substance-specific fellowships;
- recovery-community building.

No entry means THRIVE endorses one recovery path over another. Resources remain participant-directed guidance and navigation. Outside organizations remain the authority for their own programs, eligibility, schedules, meeting rules, content, and availability.

## Proposed subcategory vocabulary

These are content-organization labels only. They do not create new schema requirements.

- `meeting_finder`
- `online_meetings`
- `family_support`
- `newcomer_orientation`
- `literature_tools`
- `peer_community`
- `specialty_fellowship`
- `build_support`

## Verification cadence rule

- **fast_changing** — meeting schedules, directories, chapter locators, online access, apps, or live community listings.
- **moderate** — guidance pages, newcomer information, program descriptions, downloadable worksheets, or frequently maintained support material.
- **stable** — foundational books, core program frameworks, and durable official reference material.

## Catalog balance after adding all 34

If all entries were eventually approved and installed, the Recovery catalog would move from **6 to 40 canonical entries**.

Approximate functional mix:
- 20 find/connect entries
- 10 newcomer/read/tool entries
- 4 build/deepen entries

This is intentionally not equal by pathway. The live product's most immediate participant job is still finding real support quickly, so find/connect remains the largest slice.

## Review findings and cautions

### 1. Do not duplicate the current six
Several official organizations already represented in the live catalog also have useful additional pathways. The proposed additions are separated by user job:
- A.A. existing meeting support vs Meeting Guide app vs online-only directory.
- NA existing meeting support/literature vs virtual-only search vs virtual meeting build support.
- SMART existing meeting finder vs newcomer guidance, tools, and Family & Friends.
- Celebrate Recovery is not expanded in v0.1 because the current live record already has multiple access paths and should be inspected before creating another canonical entry.

### 2. Family resources are not evidence about the participant
Al-Anon, Nar-Anon, ACA, and SMART Family & Friends are navigation options. Their presence in the library must not imply anything about a participant's family, childhood, partner, or home situation.

### 3. Specialty fellowships should not become labels
C.A., CMA, MA, H.A., and MARA should be surfaced as optional pathways. Viewing or selecting one does not establish a diagnosis, primary substance, medication status, or recovery identity.

### 4. Youth-oriented pathways need audience discipline
Young People in Recovery is appropriate for younger adults. This candidate does not add Alateen or Narateen because THRIVE's current modeled participant environment is adult-facing and those programs have youth-specific participation rules.

### 5. Outside-world facts remain outside-world facts
Meeting schedules, access links, local availability, chapter status, and program rules can change. THRIVE should retain verification dates and point participants back to the official authority rather than presenting copied schedules as durable truth.
