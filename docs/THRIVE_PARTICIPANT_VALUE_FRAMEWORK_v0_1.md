# THRIVE Participant Value Framework v0.1

**Status:** Approved product-direction anchor  
**Purpose:** Define the participant value THRIVE must return for the effort a person gives it.  
**Current implementation gate:** Contextual Wellness Return v0.1

## Product promise

> THRIVE helps you keep track of what’s going on, figure out what matters next, and keep moving without having to hold everything in your head.

Internal operating principle:

> THRIVE turns scattered life information into continuity, choices, and follow-through.

## Core value test

Participant effort must be asymmetric: **minimum useful input, maximum useful return**.

The participant should not have to understand or maintain the machinery behind THRIVE. Authorized staff may perform structured operational work; the participant should make choices inside an environment THRIVE already remembers.

Every participant-facing interaction should answer a practical question:

> **Did THRIVE help something move?**

A screen, prompt, check-in, or reflection that collects information without returning orientation, continuity, a useful next step, or clearer understanding is incomplete.

## Burden-carrying principle

THRIVE should carry cognitive load that the participant should not have to carry alone. It should remember prior facts, unfinished items, recent choices, and relevant context so the participant does not repeatedly reconstruct their own story.

THRIVE should not ask the participant to:

- re-enter facts the system already knows;
- perform clerical work that authorized staff or the system can perform;
- repeatedly answer identical questions simply because a new day began;
- interpret raw system data without useful context;
- keep every open loop in memory in order to use the product effectively.

## Open loops

THRIVE should notice and preserve unfinished participant work across time, including goals, support requests, resource needs, money decisions, and other participant-owned follow-through.

An open loop is not a warning label. It is continuity: **what was started, what changed, what still matters, and what the participant can do next.**

## Wellness: adaptive return, not repetitive intake

Wellness interactions should change with context and history rather than behaving like a static daily form.

A wellness return may:

- acknowledge what the participant already recorded;
- compare the current entry with recent self-reported patterns;
- surface a relevant unfinished item;
- suggest one small next action;
- offer a different reflection when repeating the same question would add little value.

THRIVE must separate recorded facts from patterns, explanations, and conclusions. A pattern may be presented as something worth noticing, not as a diagnosis or hidden judgment.

## Goals: experiments and movement

Goals should behave as living experiments rather than a list of declarations.

THRIVE should help the participant see:

- what they said they wanted;
- what they tried;
- what changed;
- what helped or got in the way;
- what they want to try next.

Progress is not limited to completion. Clarifying, adjusting, pausing, or deliberately changing direction can also be useful movement when accurately represented.

## Money: decision support, not moral scoring

Financial information should help the participant understand choices, tradeoffs, timing, and follow-through.

Bank and transaction data are observational evidence only. A transaction does not establish intent, irresponsibility, relapse, incapacity, misuse, or any legal, clinical, or fiduciary conclusion.

THRIVE should return practical context such as what changed, what may affect an existing plan, and what decision may need attention next.

## Resources: contextual, not a directory dump

Resources should become more useful because THRIVE knows the participant’s current context. The system should connect an approved resource to the participant’s stated need, current goal, support request, or next step when authorized facts support that connection.

The participant should not have to browse the entire resource system to discover why something may matter today.

## Support: continuity between human contacts

Support should preserve the thread between the participant and authorized human support. THRIVE should help both sides understand what was requested, what happened, what remains unresolved, and what should be followed up without replacing human judgment or authority.

## Story layer

THRIVE’s story layer follows this order:

1. **Facts** — what the participant or an authorized source actually recorded.
2. **Pattern** — what appears to repeat or relate across those facts.
3. **Participant explanation** — what the participant says the pattern means or why it happened.
4. **Useful return** — orientation, options, or a next step grounded in those facts.

THRIVE must not fabricate explanations, consent, intent, clinical findings, or authority.

## Spiritual posture

THRIVE may be spiritually supportive and may reflect Christlike values such as dignity, service, compassion, accountability, forgiveness, stewardship, patience, and hope.

The participant experience remains **non-denominational and not Bible-forward by default**. Spiritual content should not be imposed, should not be used as a substitute for professional care, and should respect the participant’s own beliefs and preferences.

## Product-return hierarchy

The participant should increasingly experience THRIVE as a system that:

1. remembers what they should not have to remember alone;
2. notices what changed;
3. connects relevant facts across time;
4. helps identify what matters now;
5. offers a small understandable next move;
6. preserves continuity with their own goals and authorized human support.

## Phased implementation

The framework is broader than any single feature. Implementation remains gated and incremental.

### Phase 1 — Contextual Wellness Return v0.1

Use existing authorized wellness history and current participant input to return a small amount of useful context after a wellness interaction. The first version should prove that THRIVE can remember, compare, and respond without overreaching.

### Later phases

- Open-loop continuity.
- Goal experiment cycle.
- Contextual Resources.
- Story / participant explanation layer.
- Cross-surface usefulness learning based on what genuinely helps the participant move.

These later phases are **not automatically approved for implementation** by this document. Each remains subject to reconciliation, candidate review, testing, and explicit approval.

## Boundary

This framework defines participant value and product direction. It does not merge THRIVE with the Trust Engine, grant new authority, create consent, authorize external sharing, or alter database ownership. Database truth and documented authority remain controlling.
