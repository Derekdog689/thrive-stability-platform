# THRIVE Living Signal Visual System Seal
## 2026-09-30

Status: **VISUAL DIRECTION ACCEPTED / SEALED**

This checkpoint freezes the accepted participant-facing visual direction for THRIVE Living Signal after mobile review.

## Product north star

THRIVE is not primarily a tracker. THRIVE is a responsive support environment.

Visual bar:

> Calm enough to trust, alive enough to return to, direct enough to help.

The approved experience should feel like the Living Signal board became a real application, not an old THRIVE page with scenery added to it.

## What is accepted

The following are accepted as the Living Signal participant-facing visual system:

- cinematic environmental imagery;
- layered atmospheric depth;
- translucent / glass-like surfaces integrated with the environment;
- distinct visual identities for Today, Wellness, Goals, Money, Support, Resources, Story, and completion states;
- meaningful motion such as mist drift, light movement, water shimmer, progress-path illumination, and completion breathing;
- cumulative progress rather than brittle streak theater;
- completion as an earned environmental moment rather than confetti;
- Story as a narrative / journey surface rather than a report;
- progress geography as a visual metaphor for accumulated movement;
- a compact mobile navigation system with safe-area protection;
- adult, premium, supportive presentation without institutional or clinical visual language.

## Environmental choreography

Living Signal is intentionally **not** a single-landscape system.

Mountains remain a major part of the visual language because they communicate distance, movement, perspective, challenge, and achievement. They are not the only environment.

The visual system may use a controlled library of environments including:

- mountain sunrise / valley;
- alpine lake;
- South Florida coast / beach sunrise;
- calm ocean horizon;
- mangrove or coastal boardwalk;
- forest path;
- reflective water;
- evening sanctuary;
- moonlit water;
- storm clearing;
- quiet urban or neighborhood lights when appropriate.

Environmental variation must be intentional, not random wallpaper rotation.

### Emotional mapping

Typical meanings may include:

- **mountains / peaks:** movement, challenge, direction, achievement;
- **coast / beach / horizon:** release, openness, reset, breathing room;
- **water / lakes:** reflection, transition, stability, renewal;
- **sunrise:** beginning, possibility, another day;
- **evening / night:** sanctuary, decompression, review, closure;
- **paths / boardwalks / bridges:** continuation, next action, connection.

These are design cues, not rigid clinical interpretations.

## Motion

Motion is part of Living Signal now, not deferred polish.

Approved motion vocabulary includes subtle:

- mist drift;
- sunlight drift;
- water shimmer;
- atmospheric light change;
- progress-path glow;
- milestone-node movement;
- completion pulse / breathing;
- occasional distant environmental motion such as birds, clouds, wave movement, or lighthouse-like light when tasteful.

Motion must remain restrained, meaningful, and compatible with reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

The environment may occasionally acknowledge progress through a visual change. It should never become spectacle, gamification theater, or distraction.

## Lane identity

The shared Living Signal chassis remains coherent while individual lanes may carry distinct environmental and tonal treatment.

Examples:

- Today: morning / evening atmosphere and current movement;
- Wellness: calmer, lower-intensity environments;
- Goals: stronger vertical / achievement imagery and meaningful completion;
- Money: teal / reflective water and clear closeout moments;
- Recovery Support / Resources: grounded utility with lighter scenic dominance;
- Story: the broadest environmental canvas and strongest journey / geography metaphor.

## Acceptance standard

Future Living Signal work should pass this question:

> Does this feel like the application from the approved visual board?

The standard is not merely “the influence is visible.”
